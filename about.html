Our Story

Born From a Real Problem

KarigarHub was founded after experiencing firsthand how difficult it is for Pakistani families to find trustworthy home service professionals. Fake workers, overcharging, and unreliable service were common problems.

We built a platform where every karigar is verified, every price is transparent, and every customer is protected. From Karachi to Islamabad — we are Pakistan's home services solution.
