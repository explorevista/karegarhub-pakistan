Born From a Real Pakistani Problem

KarigarHub was born after Muhammad Ali experienced firsthand how hard it is for Pakistani families to find trustworthy home professionals. Fake workers, overcharging, and unreliable service were everywhere.

We built a platform where every karigar is background-checked, every price is shown upfront, and every customer is protected by our service guarantee. From Karachi to Islamabad — we are Pakistan's home services solution.
